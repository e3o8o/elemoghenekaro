Elem Oghenekaro

I'm building Parametrig, a research and engineering company developing insurance infrastructure for the programmable economy. The work focuses on pricing models and reserve operations, along with payout systems for measurable failures.

Legitimate Intervention Framework (LIF) is an open research initiative I'm leading on incident response and governance in decentralized systems, with special attention to recovery. The current work includes a growing dataset of over 705-exploit cases, a paper on legitimate overrides, and practical framework design for bounded emergency action.

Previously, I led research on stablecoin payments at Orbital and helped build the first governance aggregator for Web3 at Messari. I also contributed to the launch and governance design of Aragon Network DAO.
